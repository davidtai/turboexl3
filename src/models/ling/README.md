# Ling-3.1-flash

The code only Ling-3.1-flash uses, on the core's routed chain, DeepSeek-V4.1's banked decode and the core's dense linear:

| file | what it holds |
|---|---|
| `pair.zig` | `linearPair` (a same-input pair, each member in its own buffers, in one DEC-ONE launch: the prepare inside, or one prepare launch for both members then the GEMV over their rows), each member's `linear` bytes |
| `kdaproj.zig` | `kdaProj` (a KDA layer's q|k|v, f and g in one DEC-ONE GEMV launch after one prepare launch, each member in its own buffers at its own K split), `kdaProjPrepared` (the GEMV alone over a host's prepared rows), `ncntFor`; each member's `linear` bytes; `kdaProjB` adds the layer's b_proj (bf16) as a fourth member of the GEMV launch, MLX's gemv over each row (`kernels/kdaproj/bmember.metal`) |
| `kdaproj_pf.zig` | `kdaProjPrompt` (a KDA layer's q|k|v, f and g at prompt rows: each member's prepare and W_hat by its `linear`'s launches, then the three products and finishes in one launch, pg2's fused NAX GEMM and finish over the members), `takes`, `tileFor`; each member's `linear` bytes |
| `kernels/kdaproj/` | the KDA projections' pieces: the prepare's member choice, DEC-ONE's head over three members (each member's widths and K split), the member's prepared rows, trellis, svh, output, partial sums and counters; the prompt-row launch's member choice, its column and the macro's end around pg2's pieces (`pf_*.metal`) |
| `kernels/pair/` | the pair's pieces: the prepare's member choice (its suh, its rows), the GEMVs' member choice (its prepared rows or its suh) and the member's trellis, output tiles and svh |
| `api.zig` | `moeLing` (the routed experts with Ling's SwiGLU: none, or clamped after the SiLU) and `moeLingBanked` with its halves (the same over an expert streamer's three slot banks); the combine's entries |
| `ling_test.zig` | the texts' compositions, the reference clamp, the refusals and the trunk shapes (CPU); `moeLing` against `moe` and the reference, `moeLingBanked` against `moeLing` byte for byte, `linear` at every trunk shape, a one-row call on the one-row table's launch (`Config.dec_one_row_table`, whose per-shape choice the CPU tests pin) against one row's own and the three-launch chain byte for byte, and the pair against each member's `linear` byte for byte in both of its forms (GPU) |
| `shared.zig` | `sharedExpert` (the shared expert at decode rows: the gate and the up in one DEC-ONE launch, each in its own buffers, whose pairs of output blocks run the host's SwiGLU and the down's prepare, then the down's DEC-ONE GEMV), `sharedTakes`, `sigmoidTable` (the host's tables) |
| `shared_test.zig` | the joint texts' compositions and pinned statements (CPU); `sharedExpert` against the composed chain word for word at rows 1 to 4, limits 0 and 10 (GPU) |
| `kernels/shared/` | the pieces the shared expert's texts add to dense's DEC-ONE: the member choice and its tile address, the member's store, the pair's arrival, the two SwiGLUs and the down's prepare; `kernels/test/pins/` what the CPU test pins |
| `swiglu.zig` | `upSwiglu` (the prefill SwiGLU epilogue: up's inner product by `linear`'s own launches at prompt rows, then its finish running the host's SwiGLU of the gate's value and its own), `upSwigluW` (the same over a W_hat the caller decoded once, at the matmul path's rows), `takes` (where it serves), the two texts (dense's `finish_v`, the store loop's opening and close in `kernels/swiglu/`, the shared expert's SwiGLU statements) |
| `combine.zig` | `combineRouted` and `combineParts` (a wide routed call's combine in one launch, over a joined array or the call's unjoined outputs), the texts' CPU test |
| `combine_test.zig` | both against MLX's composed combine word for word, the refusals (GPU) |
| `kernels/combine/` | the combine's pieces: the contract pragma, the source choice, the fold's head, each text's row read, the fold and store |
| `router.zig` | `route`, `routerLogits`, `select` and `tableF32` (a MoE layer's router at decode rows 1 to 8 in two launches: MLX's one-row gemv over the host loader's column-major table for the logits, or over the loaded f16 weight widened then + 0, beside its gemv / gemv_wide for the read-ahead predictor's scores, then the host's fused router text over the logits with its weights in bf16; the loader's f32 table rebuilt from the f16 weight), the texts' and the plan's CPU tests |
| `router_test.zig` | the logits, both predictor scores, the ids and the weights against the host's served path word for word at rows 1 to 8 (every operand built as the host builds it: the loaded f16 weight, the loader's column-major table, the MoE input, denseRows), a weight holding every finite f16 word, the rebuilt table at the stock widths, with `TURBOEXL3_LING_PACK=<pack dir>` every router weight of the pack as stored (the MoE layers' and the NextN layer's), the refusals (GPU) |
| `bproj_test.zig` | `kdaProjB`'s b_proj member against the served b_proj (the one-row matmul, the exact verify's denseRows) and its three members against `kdaProj`'s word for word at rows 1 to 8, operands as the loader keeps them; the refusals (GPU); the member's text and gemv statements (CPU) |
| `kdaproj_pf_test.zig` | `kdaProjPrompt`'s members against each `root.linear` word for word at prompt rows 33 to 16384, operands as the loader keeps them, the refusal at 32 rows (GPU); its timing against the three `linear` calls over a short tile sweep (TURBOEXL3_LING_TIME) |
| `rows_test.zig` | `moeLingRows` against the outside plugin's prompt chunk (take, bf16, f32, `moeLing` at top-1) byte for byte on the loader's views; the routed chain's variants (`variants`: the 256-row tiles, the remainder cut, the gate and up GEMM's column blocks, the fused finish, also only for the experts of at least 64, 80 or 96 rows, the plain launches) against the served engine word for word on synthetic calls at every row bucket, both sides of each threshold, a lognormal-popularity call and (the threshold arms) a ladder of rows per expert, and ten red checks caught: a flipped word, four planted kernel faults (`kernels/test/fault/`: a value, a route, two pure reorderings), a rounding change, each half of the threshold's launch marked in exactly its rows, and a threshold ignored or off by one (GPU); the rows path's and each variant's timing (TURBOEXL3_LING_TIME) |
| `swiglu_test.zig` | `upSwiglu` against the composed chain (the gate's and the up's `linear`, the host's SwiGLU as MLX's ops) word for word at the shared expert's and the dense layers' shapes, rows 17 to 16384, limits 0 and 10, its declines and refusals (GPU); `upSwigluW` over one decoded W_hat against `upSwiglu` word for word at the matmul path's rows, its declines and refusals (GPU); the texts' composition and pinned statements (CPU); its timing (TURBOEXL3_LING_TIME) |
| `kernels/router/` | the router's pieces: launch 1's head, MLX's gemv statements cut at the table's read (an f32 word, or an f16 word widened then + 0), the predictor's branch (gemv at one row, gemv_wide at 2 to 8), the two scores (headers) and the host's fused router text (`select.metal`) |
| `mla_latent.zig` | `decodeLatent` (`mlaLatentDecode`): the MLA attention at decode rows in the latent, three launches (the query absorbed into the latent, the attention's partials over the latent cache, the merge and the value half); launch 2's three variants, each launch 2's words and a host's opt-in in `Config` (`Attn`: 2 or 4 rows a threadgroup, the cache staged in f32, more tiles a simdgroup), `decodeLatentWith` and `decodeLatentParts` (a chosen text, launch 2's partials kept); its texts and plan (CPU tests) |
| `mla_latent_test.zig` | the host's f64 reference, absorbed against expanded (CPU); `decodeLatent` against it within tolerance, a call's rows against one-row calls and strided cache views against contiguous ones, byte for byte; launch 2's variants against launch 2, the output and the partials word for word on the plugin's operands, with red checks (GPU) |
| `kernels/mla_latent/` | the three launches' texts and their header, launch 2 in four pieces (`attn_1` to `attn_4`: its scores, the online softmax update the variants share, the weights times the latents, the outputs) and its variants' pieces (`rows_*`, `stage_*`, `block_*`); `kernels/test/pins/` what the CPU tests pin |
| `mla_flash.zig` | `mlaFlashPrefill` (`api.zig` exports it): the MLA prompt attention as one causal flash launch, MLX's NAX attention kernel at Ling's head dims (qk 192, v 128) |
| `mla_rows.zig` | `mlaRows` (`mlaRowsTakes`; `mlaRowsWith` at a chosen cap, `mlaRowsOn` on a test's kernel): the MLA chain's plain kv_b and head gate at decode and verify rows 1 to 16 in one GEMV launch, MLX's one-row gemv statements at MLX's tile for each row, each weight tile read once for up to `Config.mla_rows` rows (`tileFor`, `planFor`); its texts and plan (CPU tests) |
| `mla_rows_test.zig` | `mlaRows` against the served launches (the one-row matmul, the plugin's denseRows) word for word at kv_b's and the head gate's shapes, rows 1 to 8, 12 and 16, caps 1 to 8, on random rows and on built rows an order change moves, weights as the loader keeps them; four red checks (two planted reorderings, a changed weight word, swapped rows); the configuration's path and the refusals (GPU); `servedCall` and `loaderWeight` for the timing |
| `kernels/mla_rows/` | the launch's pieces: MLX's gemv statements up to the accumulate (`head`), the accumulate (`acc`), the K loop's close and the sums (`simd`), the threadgroup sum (`tgsum`) and the store; `kernels/test/fault/mla_rows_*` the red checks' reorderings |
| `mla_flash_test.zig` | the flash kernel's text (CPU) and its standalone translation units (TURBOEXL3_METAL_DUMP=1); against MLX's fused SDPA word for word where MLX instantiates one, and within tolerance of an f64 reference at Ling's heads (GPU) |
| `kernels/mla_flash/` | the flash kernel's pieces: MLX's attention_nax body less its unused arms, its row ops, the kernel attribute's define and undefine; `kernels/test/` its pin file and dump data |

Ling's own Metal is the pairs', the shared expert's, the routed combine's and the router's pieces (`kernels/pair/`,
`kernels/shared/`, `kernels/combine/`, `kernels/router/`),
the MLA attention in the latent's three launches (`kernels/mla_latent/`), the MLA chain's rows GEMV (`kernels/mla_rows/`)
and the MLA prompt attention's flash kernel (`kernels/mla_flash/`;
`kernels/test/pins/` what the CPU tests pin); the routed experts' clamp pieces are the core's
(`kernels/decode/fin7_swiglu_clamp_out.metal`, `kernels/prefill/mid_swiglu_clamp_out.metal`). docs/models/ling/
describes the model's needs and its kernels.
